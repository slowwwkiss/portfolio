About

I am Achmad Reza Baehaqiii, from Bogor Regency in West Java, Indonesia. I graduated from a vocational high school in office administration, then taught myself to build software. Today my main interests are AI training and data annotation, and I spend most of my time shipping real projects with a team of AI agents that I direct.

I work in short, intense sprints. I set a clear goal, break it into small steps, and keep pushing until the thing runs in front of real users. I do not collect half finished demos. Every project on this page was built in November 2026, and every one of them actually works.

“Ga kerja gua pecut” is my team motto. It means: if the work is not moving, I crack the whip, even on myself.
